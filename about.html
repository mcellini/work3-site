The people behind Work3

Written by people studying work from inside the change.

Work3 is led by Matteo and Andy, two writers tracking how AI changes the structure of work — not only the tools people use, but the jobs, companies, skills, incentives, and institutions those tools reshape.

M

Matteo Cellini

Matteo is a Rome-based writer and operator focused on AI-native work, agentic organizations, career systems, skills, and the emerging infrastructure layer underneath modern work.

His Work3 writing connects technological change with management design, labor markets, platform power, and the lived reality of people trying to build leverage in a system that is moving faster than its institutions.

  • Core lens: AI-native work and the post-job economy
  • Recurring questions: what replaces jobs, what becomes a skill, and who owns the work graph?
  • Mode: essays, frameworks, research notes, and field guides
A

Andy

Andy is one of Work3’s main writers, bringing a complementary view on how organizations, talent systems, markets, and technology adoption change when AI becomes part of the operating layer of work.

His contribution helps Work3 stay grounded in practical consequences: what leaders need to understand, what teams need to change, and what the next wave of work infrastructure makes possible or dangerous.

  • Core lens: organizations, strategy, and adoption
  • Recurring questions: how should teams change, where does value move, and what should leaders do now?
  • Mode: analysis, briefings, and practical translation

Why Work3 exists

Most AI commentary focuses on tools. Work3 focuses on the system-level consequences: how work is coordinated, valued, owned, and remembered when execution becomes programmable.

We write for operators, founders, executives, people leaders, policy thinkers, and ambitious workers who need a sharper map of the transition.
