The positioning

Not another AI newsletter.

Work3 studies the shift from jobs and software tools to systems of agents, skills, workflows, and organizational memory — and asks who captures the value when work itself becomes infrastructure.

Work3 connects technology, labor, management, policy, and culture — because the future of work is not one market. It is a new stack.
